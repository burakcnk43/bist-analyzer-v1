# 📈 BCBIST AI

### AI-Powered Financial Analysis Platform for Borsa Istanbul

BCBIST AI is an intelligent financial analysis platform designed to help investors better understand companies listed on Borsa Istanbul.

Using artificial intelligence, technical indicators, and financial statement analysis, BCBIST AI transforms complex market data into clear and actionable reports.

---

## 🚀 Live Demo

🌐 https://bcbistv1.streamlit.app

---

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 AI-powered stock analysis
- 📊 Technical indicator analysis
- 📈 Financial statement evaluation
- 📉 Company comparison
- 📰 Market insights
- 📋 AI-generated financial reports
- ⚡ Fast and interactive dashboard

## 🎯 Why I Built This

Many beginner investors struggle to understand financial statements and technical indicators.

BCBIST AI simplifies this process by combining AI with financial analysis, making investing easier to understand.

---

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

BCBIST AI is designed for educational and research purposes only.

It does not provide financial or investment advice.

Users should always conduct their own research before making investment decisions.
